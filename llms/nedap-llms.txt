# Nedap

> Nedap is a leader in Digital Twin Technology, bridging the physical and digital worlds in Healthcare, Livestock, Retail, and Security. Through our Technology for Life philosophy, we create sustainable, forward-thinking solutions that help people and organizations succeed in an ever-changing world.
> Nedap has a workforce of over 1,000 employees and operates on a global scale. The company was founded in 1929 and has been listed on Euronext Amsterdam since 1947. Its headquarters is located in Groenlo, the Netherlands.

## Markets and solutions

- [Nedap in Healthcare](https://www.nedap.com/en/healthcare): Electronic health record solutions that help care professionals spend more time with the people in their care.
- [Nedap in Livestock](https://www.nedap.com/en/livestock): Cow monitoring technology that supports animal health, welfare, farm performance, and sustainable dairy farming.
- [Nedap in Retail](https://www.nedap.com/en/retail): Item-level inventory visibility and connected loss prevention for unified commerce.
- [Nedap in Security](https://www.nedap.com/en/security): Access management and identification solutions for secure, seamless movement.
